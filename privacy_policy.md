## 2. Processed Data & Purpose (Art. 6(1)(b) & (f) GDPR)
Laserbot processes data exclusively to provide team and tournament management features. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
- **Discord Identifiers:** User ID, username/display name, Guild ID, Channel ID, and Message ID to isolate server data and update event embeds.
- **Profile & Travel Logistics:** Optional driver's license status, room preferences, roommate/passenger preferences, and per-tournament travel responses (arrival/departure day, accommodation) to organize hotels and carpools.
- **Absences & Reminders:** User-submitted vacation periods (for automatic event declines) and personal DM reminders (deleted automatically upon delivery).
- **Laserforce Tags & Notes:** Voluntarily linked iPlayLaserforce tags (visible only on servers you share with the Bot) and short event notes.
- **Moderation Records:** Server-level or global bot restrictions (User ID, expiration, reason) stored to prevent abuse.

## 3. Anonymous Elections & Secret Ballots
Captain elections (`/captainwahl`), team decisions (`/aufnahme`, `/votekick`), and anonymous polls (`/vote`) hash voter IDs irreversibly using **SHA-256** combined with a secret cryptographic salt. Individual secret votes cannot be reconstructed.

## 4. Self-Service GDPR Rights (Art. 15 & Art. 17 GDPR)
You have full self-service control over your data directly inside Discord (on any server or via Direct Message to Laserbot using `/privacy` or `/profile`):
- **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR):** Click **"GDPR Data Export"** to immediately download a complete, cross-server report of all personal data linked to your Discord User ID.
- **Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR):** Click **"Delete All My Data"** to permanently erase all profile, travel, absence, reminder, tag, and participation records across all servers.
- Alternatively, you may contact the Data Controller (Section 1) providing your numeric Discord User ID.
